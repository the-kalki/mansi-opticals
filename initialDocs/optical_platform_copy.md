### 1.4 High-Intent FAQ Section
1. **H3: Can I upload an expired prescription?**
   * **Answer**: We strictly require a valid, unexpired prescription to ensure your eye health. If yours is expired, easily book a fast, certified eye exam with our in-house optometrists today!
2. **H3: How do I get my PD (Pupillary Distance)?**
   * **Answer**: No ruler needed! Use our Instant Auto-PD tool during checkout. Your device's camera will measure your PD with clinical accuracy in under 10 seconds.
3. **H3: What is the difference between single-vision and progressive lenses?**
   * **Answer**: Single-vision corrects for one distance (near or far). Our digital progressives offer a seamless, line-free transition from distance to intermediate to reading vision.
4. **H3: Do you accept HSA/FSA and vision insurance?**
   * **Answer**: Absolutely. You can use your HSA/FSA cards directly at checkout. We also provide itemized receipts to easily submit for out-of-network insurance reimbursement.
5. **H3: What does your warranty cover?**
   * **Answer**: Every pair comes with a 1-Year Frame & Lens Warranty. If your frames break or your lenses get scratched under normal use, we replace them—zero hassle.
6. **H3: Can I cancel or reschedule my eye exam?**
   * **Answer**: Yes, you can reschedule or cancel up to 24 hours before your slot via your patient dashboard without any penalties.
7. **H3: Do you offer a home trial before buying?**
   * **Answer**: We offer a 30-Day Zero-Risk Return Policy rather than home trials. Use our hyper-realistic AR Virtual Mirror, order your pair, and if the fit isn't perfect, return them for free.
8. **H3: What is the turnaround time for prescription glasses?**
   * **Answer**: Lab processing takes 3–5 business days for single vision, and 5–7 days for complex progressives. Express dispatch ensures they reach your door fast and secure.
